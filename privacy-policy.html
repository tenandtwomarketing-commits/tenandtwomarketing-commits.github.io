Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026.

This policy covers the "MCC2 Ads" application operated by Ten and Two Marketing.

What the application does

MCC2 Ads connects to the Google Ads API to read campaign, keyword, search term and conversion data for Google Ads accounts that Ten and Two Marketing manages on behalf of its clients, and to apply account changes that Ten and Two Marketing staff have approved.

Who uses it

The application is used only by Ten and Two Marketing staff, signing in with Ten and Two Marketing's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

Through the Google Ads API scope, the application reads advertising performance data and account settings. It does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google service.

How data is stored and shared

Data retrieved from Google Ads is stored on Ten and Two Marketing's own computers for reporting and analysis. It is shared only with the client whose account it belongs to. It is not sold or passed to any other third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.
